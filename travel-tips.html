Travel Tips

Essential Advice for Exploring El Nido, Palawan

Plan Smart, Travel Safe

Make the most of your island getaway with practical advice on transportation, seasonal weather, eco-fees, and sustainable travel practices in El Nido.

Getting to El Nido

Fly directly to Lio Airport (ENI) from Manila or Cebu for the fastest arrival, or land at Puerto Princesa International Airport (PPS) and take a scenic 5- to 6-hour van or bus ride north through Palawan.

Best Time to Visit

The dry season from November to May offers calm seas and sunny skies ideal for island hopping. March to May brings the warmest temperatures, while June to October is quieter with occasional tropical rain showers.

Getting Around

Tricycles are the primary mode of transportation for getting around the town proper. For exploring nearby beaches like Nacpan or Duli, renting a scooter or hiring a private van is recommended.

What to Pack

Bring a waterproof dry bag for phones and cameras, aqua shoes for sharp limestone rocks, reef-safe sunscreen, a reusable water bottle, and plenty of cash—ATMs in town can occasionally run out.

Important Visitor Reminders

Eco-Tourism Fee

Pay the mandatory ₱400 Environmental Tourism Development Fee at the port or tour office before boarding.

No Single-Use Plastics

Disposable plastic bottles, bags, and straws are strictly banned on all El Nido island hopping tours.

Book Tours Early

Reserve Tours A, B, C, or D at least a day ahead, especially during peak holiday months.
