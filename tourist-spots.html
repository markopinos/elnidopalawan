Tour A • Miniloc Island

Big Lagoon

Barangay Bebeladan, El Nido

A breathtaking natural wonder famous for its crystal-clear emerald and turquoise waters enclosed by towering, dramatic vertical limestone cliffs. Paddling through its calm channels offers an unforgettable serene experience.

  • Kayak through crystal-clear turquoise channels
  • View 100-meter vertical karst formations
  • Spot reef fish in shallow sunlit waters
Mainland • North El Nido

Nacpan Beach

Sitio Calitang, Barangay Bucana

A stunning four-kilometer stretch of powdery golden-white sand fringed by swaying coconut palms. Known as one of Palawan's finest beaches, it boasts gentle waves and legendary sunset views.

  • Walk along 4 km of unspoiled white-sand shoreline
  • Hike the hill viewpoint overlooking Twin Beaches
  • Watch golden sunsets over the West Philippine Sea
Tour C • Matinloc Island

Hidden Beach

Matinloc Island, Bacuit Bay

Tucked away behind jagged limestone karst walls, this secret paradise is accessed by swimming or wading through a narrow rock crevice into a secluded, sun-drenched lagoon.

  • Swim through a natural limestone opening into a secret cove
  • Snorkel over vibrant shallow coral gardens
  • Relax on powder-fine white sand shielded from waves

Visitor Guidelines

Eco-Tourism Fee

Secure your Environmental Tourism Development Fee (₱400), valid for 5 days across all islands.

Reef-Safe Gear

Bring aqua shoes for sharp limestone rocks, a dry bag for electronics, and reef-safe sunscreen.

Zero Plastics

Single-use plastics are strictly prohibited on island tours—bring a refillable water bottle.

El Nido, Palawan. Always confirm current fees and rules with the local tourism office before you travel.
